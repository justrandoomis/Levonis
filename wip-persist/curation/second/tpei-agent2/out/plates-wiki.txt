## ¶ Build Plate
The build plate is one of the core accessories of an FDM 3D printer. It is magnetically secured to the printer's heatbed and serves as the platform that supports the model. During printing, filament is extruded and stacked layer by layer, and the build plate provides a stable adhesion surface for the extruded filament, ensuring that the model does not shift or warp. In addition, the build plate features a flexible design, so once printing is complete, you can simply flex the plate to quickly and easily remove the print.

Build Plate Cleaning: During use, the build plate needs to be cleaned regularly to maintain optimal adhesion. For detailed cleaning instructions, see: Textured PEI Plate Cleaning Guide.
Important Notice:
1.The 256×256 mm Textured PEI Plate, Smooth PEI Plate, and Cool Plate SuperTack are available in two generations.
First-generation plates are compatible with A1 and X1/P1 series printers.
Second-generation plates are compatible with the P2S/X2D printer.
The main difference lies in the updated QR code identification.
⚠️ First-generation build plates do not support the build plate recognition feature of second-generation printers (P2S and newer).
2.P2S/X2D printers can physically use first-generation build plates, but the plate type will not be automatically recognized.
If the printer displays a “Build plate not recognized” message, select “Resolved” on the screen or disable the Build Plate Detection feature.
3.X1 series printers can also use second-generation build plates, but they will not be automatically identified.
If the printer shows an unrecognized plate warning, choose “Resolved” or disable Build Plate Detection in the settings.
4.A1 and P1 series printers are fully compatible with second-generation build plates.

The Textured PEI Plate is manufactured by spraying PEI powder onto both sides of a stainless steel sheet. It is the standard plate included with Bambu lab printers. Its main features are as follows:
Special textured surface: Adds a unique textured finish to the bottom surface of printed parts.
Strong adhesion: In most cases, it provides excellent adhesion without the need for an adhesive.
Durable, long-lasting coating: The PEI surface coating is highly durable and robust.

## ¶ Features
✅ Excellent First-layer Adhesion and Improved Durability
After optimizing the manufacturing process of the Bambu Texture PEI Plate, the durability of the plate is enhanced. Additionally, the adhesion between the prints and the plate is significantly improved, eliminating the need for adhesives.
[img:]
✅ Self-release when Cooled Down
PEI is an excellent material for 3D printing applications due to its ability to withstand the repeated heating and cooling cycles of a printer heatbed. Prints come off easily when the temperature of the heatbed reaches room temperature, and we always recommend waiting until it reaches 35℃ or lower. If the print is still adheres to the plate, simply bend the steel plate and the model will fall right into your hands.
[img:]
✅ Compatible with Various Filaments
PEI enables printing not only with common materials such as PLA, TPU, PETG, but also with materials that require high temperatures like ABS and Nylon.
[img:]
When printing PC/PA/ABS/ASA filaments on a Textured PEI Plate, it may be necessary to apply a layer of glue stick.
Further Reading:
 Bambu Lab Textured PEI Plate Cleaning Guide
The Smooth PEI Plate is obtained by adhering a carefully selected PEI sheet to a spring steel using a highly heat-resistant 3M adhesive. The Smooth PEI Plate provides a flat surface for printed objects and is suitable for scenarios that require a level bottom surface.
The PEI sheet on the surface of the Smooth PEI Plate allows for printing with various filaments.
Only PLA filament does not require glue, while printing with other filaments requires the use of gluing to prevent the PEI sheet from tearing.
The thicker 0.5 mm spring steel helps reduce the risk of warping in the printing plate.

The stainless steel plate is equipped with dual-sided coatings, one side featuring textured PEI and the other side featuring smooth PEI. Users can choose between different surface textures, allowing the model's bottom surface to have either a matte or smooth finish, providing more printing options.

The Engineering Plate is manufactured using a corrosion-resistant reinforced coating process, specifically optimized for printing engineering materials such as ABS, ASA, PC, and PA. Compared with traditional build plates that tend to suffer from coating degradation over time, the new flat build plate significantly enhances surface corrosion resistance and durability. Its fine and uniform surface texture ensures stable adhesion while providing printed parts with a smoother, nearly glossy bottom finish.
ℹ️ Note:
1.Before printing with any filament, apply glue on the Engineering Plate. Depending on the specific material and conditions, choose either a glue stick or liquid adhesive. It is recommended to refer to the official Bambu Lab Glue Application Guide for proper usage.
2.Exercise caution when using a scraper to remove prints from the build plate. If the printed model adheres too tightly to the plate, apply alcohol to the contact area between the model and the build plate to ease removal. Avoid using excessive force to prevent injury or damage to the model or the build plate.

## ¶ FAQs
👇 Click on the problem to see the corresponding solution:
 ▸ Model Detachment During Printing
Solution: Apply a uniform layer of liquid glue before printing to enhance adhesion. For detailed instructions on glue application, please refer to the official Bambu Lab Glue Application Guide.
 ▸ White Marks on the Model Bottom After Printing
Solution: Whitening on the model bottom is usually caused by the solidification of glue or glue stick residue during heating. Wipe the bottom of the printed model with a cleaning agent or alcohol to remove any residue and eliminate whitening marks.
 ▸ Residual Print Marks on the Plate Surface
Solution: After prolonged use with certain materials, color variations or faint print marks may appear on the surface of the plate. This is normal and does not affect performance. Most marks can be removed by:
Cleaning with detergent and water
Wiping with alcohol
 ▸ Build Plate Recognition Failure
Solution:
Make sure the QR code on the build plate is clean and undamaged.
Update the printer firmware to the latest version.
(If the issue persists, you may disable the Build Plate Recognition feature in settings and continue using the plate.)
The Cool Plate SuperTack features a finely textured surface that approaches a smooth, glossy finish. It is a beginner-friendly build plate, especially suitable for glue-free printing with PLA and PETG filaments. The surface is resistant to dirt, provides strong adhesion, and minimizes print detachment.
Even at a low bed temperature of just 40 °C, PLA prints adhere firmly to the plate, for the 2nd-generation Cool Plate SuperTack, PETG only requires a bed temperature of 55–60 °C. Please note that some printer models currently still use shared parameters for both the 1st- and 2nd-generation plates. As a result, when printing PETG on these machines (such as the X/P series), the recommended bed temperature may still be around 65 °C. This helps effectively reduce warping and surface defects such as “elephant foot,” particularly for large models where it significantly lowers the risk of warping.
In addition, the coating greatly reduces the energy required for bed heating, lowers the risk of nozzle clogging when printing PLA and PETG, decreases electricity consumption, and is more environmentally friendly.
⚠️ Note: This plate is designed only for printing PLA and PETG. Using any other materials may damage the surface coating.

## ¶ FAQs
👇 Click on the problem to see the corresponding solution:
 ▸ Printed Model Adheres Too Firmly and Is Difficult to Remove
Solutions:
Place the build plate back on the heated bed and warm it to around 50 °C, then try removing the model again.
Alternatively, apply alcohol to the underside of the plate where it contacts the model, and gently lift the model using a scraper.
For future prints, slightly lower the bed temperature according to the filament’s characteristics to reduce adhesion.
Common scenarios where removal can be difficult:
Prints with extensive tree supports and brims.
Large, flat, low-layer models such as art panels or lithophanes.
For these cases, the Cool Plate SuperTack helps improve first-layer quality and success rate even at lower temperatures. However, due to structural characteristics, such models are generally not suitable for removal by flexing the plate. It’s recommended to use the scraper-assisted methods above to reduce removal difficulty.
 ▸ Scratches on the Plate Surface from the Scraper
Explanation & Recommendation:
To achieve stable, glue-free adhesion at low temperatures, the Cool Plate SuperTack uses a coating slightly softer than that of the Textured PEI Plate, making it more susceptible to scratches.
Minor scratches from normal use will not significantly affect adhesion performance.
Best practice:
Before using a scraper, moisten the contact area between the model and the plate with alcohol. This greatly reduces removal difficulty and minimizes the risk of surface damage.
 ▸ Residual Marks on the Plate Surface
Solutions:
Most residue or marks can be removed with alcohol wipes or detergent and water.
A few persistent stains or first-layer marks are normal and do not affect long-term use or performance.
 ▸ Build Plate Recognition Failure
Solutions:
Ensure the QR code on the build plate is clean, undamaged, and unobstructed.
Update the printer firmware to the latest version.
If the issue persists, you can disable the Build Plate Recognition feature in the printer settings and continue printing normally.

During the printing process, the high-temperature polymer flows into these textures, transferring them to the bottom surface of the printed model. ## ¶ Considerations
We recommend using PLA and TPU filament for printing.
For best results, we recommend applying the 3D effect spare sheet only on the official steel plate from Bambu Lab.
Please set the plate type to Smooth PEI/High Temp Plate and carefully follow the Heatbed Recommended Settings when using the Bambu 3D Effect Plate.
Due to reflections, the LIDAR calibration will not work as expected when using Effect Sheets. We recommend skipping the LIDAR calibration sequence, or calibrating with a regular plate.
The 3D Effect Spare Sheets are considered consumables. To prolong the lifespan of the transfer textures, try placing your print objects on different areas of the sheet.

## ¶ Wipe zone on the build plate
Before printing, there may be some filament residue remaining on the nozzle, and if not cleaned, it can cause surface defects on the printed object. Therefore, the nozzle is first wiped on a nozzle wiper before printing starts and then repeatedly wiped in a specific area on the build plate (wipe zone). This helps to clean the filament residue on the nozzle and stabilize the chamber pressure inside the nozzle. This prevents surface defects on the printed object due to filament residue on the nozzle.
For the X1C, P1S/P1P, and A1 printers, as the nozzle comes into direct contact with the build plate, there may be some "wear" observed in the wiping zone (as shown in the image below). However, this phenomenon does not affect the functionality of the nozzle or the build plate.
[img:]
For the A1 mini printer, there is a wipe zone on the heatbed to clean the nozzle. Therefore, the phenomenon of "wear" on the build plate will not happen.
[img:]
## ¶ Correct gluing
For the correct gluing procedure, please check How to Use Bambu Lab Glue. Ensure that the print adheres properly to the plate without causing damage to the surface by sticking too tightly. Otherwise, the sheet may bubble or crack. Also, third-party glues are strongly discouraged and may damage the plate.
Alternatively, we provide textured PEI Plates that allow you to print without the need for a glue stick.
